Enhanced TOPMODEL on six watersheds

A browser port of the daily Enhanced TOPMODEL from the R Shiny app, run on six small US basins from wet maritime to desert and alpine. Change a parameter and the hydrograph, water balance and fit statistics update.

    About the model, data and limits

    Model. Daily Enhanced TOPMODEL: Penman-Monteith PET with a seasonal LAI curve, canopy rain and snow interception, degree-day snowmelt, optional shallow stormflow zone, infiltration excess and impervious area. The formulation is a line-by-line port of enhanced_topmodel_core() from the R app, without the land-use-change scenario and channel routing. Water balance closes to about 1e-11 mm over five years at every site, with all options on or off. I could not run the R app here, so the port is checked by that closure and by reading it against the source, not by comparing outputs.

    Data. Forcing is Daymet v4 daily weather (Thornton et al., doi:10.3334/ORNLDAAC/2129), averaged over 16 pixels sampled inside each basin. Observed flow is USGS NWIS daily mean discharge converted to mm/d with the gauge drainage area. Basin outlines come from the USGS NLDI. The topographic index is computed from the 30 m USGS 3DEP DEM with priority-flood filling, MD-infinity flow routing and ln(a/tanβ), grouped into 40 classes. The R app uses 5 m by default, so classes here are smoother and the wet tail is probably under-resolved.

    • Period is 2009–2013 on Daymet's 365-day calendar (Dec 31 dropped in 2012). Dates after Feb 2012 can be one day off the civil date. 2009 is spin-up and excluded from statistics by default.
    • Basin-mean Daymet precipitation is biased in steep terrain, so some water-balance misfit (Big Sur, Sabino Creek) comes from the forcing and cannot be fixed with soil parameters.
    • No LAI or albedo time series: LAI follows the parameter curve. No channel routing: Qsim is the sum of runoff components on the same day.
    • Preset parameters were fitted to all of 2010–2013 with a random search (11 parameters, objective = ½KGE + ½√NSE minus a bias penalty), so preset statistics are in-sample. A split-sample test (fit 2010–11, test 2012–13) is shown below the statistics table.
    • Parameters are effective values for a lumped daily model and are not uniquely identifiable from one hydrograph.

    References: Beven & Kirkby (1979) TOPMODEL; Scanlon et al. (2000) shallow stormflow; Seibert & McGlynn (2007) MD-infinity; Hedstrom & Pomeroy (1998) canopy snow; Allen et al. (1998) FAO-56; Gupta et al. (2009) KGE.
